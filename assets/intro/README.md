# Cinematic intro PNG assets

The root `index.html` launches a persistent shell containing a six-slide 960×540 film intro. The fixed credit slide lasts 7.4 seconds, the centered story prologue lasts 11.5 seconds, and four illustrated 7.4-second slides are shuffled afterward. The first interaction enables audio without skipping; a later interaction skips to the game.

The generated credit slide is always first and the procedural localized story prologue is always second. Every frame begins with an opaque dark backing fill so transparent external layers never expose the page behind the cinematic. The three supplied ships are shuffled independently across the three flight slides; the fourth illustrated slide is a dedicated destroyed-tank battlefield.

New ship files are `intro_ship01.png` (325×120), `intro_ship02.png` (269×120), and `intro_ship03.png` (259×120). Every ship receives procedural multi-layer reactor plumes, white-hot cores, cyan plasma particles, and optional intermittent twin-laser fire.

# Spaceship art contract

Ships face right, use a centered pivot, and contain no baked exhaust, glow, laser, background, or text. Those effects remain generated by the intro engine. The generated prototype is retained only as a loading fallback.

# Layer composition rules

- The cinematic canvas is 960×540, 16:9.
- Opaque backgrounds belong only in the sky/star layers.
- Mountains, planet, dunes and asteroids require valid transparency.
- Do not bake film text or game title into PNG layers.
- Do not bake fades, letterboxing, sand particles, moving stars or iris transitions into layer files.
- Use sRGB.
- Avoid a white matte around transparent art.
- Keep the center readable for temporary film text.

Text-safe regions:

```text
Slide 1 credits: approximately X 195–765, Y 215–287
Slide 2 title:   approximately X 135–825, Y 200–330
```
